Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026

Your ride videos

Equuz analyzes videos you select or record on your device. Your saved rides include the video, analysis, edits and any details you add, such as a rider or horse name. Knoet Studio does not receive your videos for analysis. Equuz has no advertising or third-party analytics.

Camera, motion and Photos

Equuz uses the camera when you record a ride. Motion data helps level the camera, and the app saves its tilt with your recording. You can select a video to import or choose to save a recording to Photos. You can change app permissions in your device’s Settings.

iCloud and sharing

If you turn on iCloud sync, Equuz stores your saved rides in your private Apple iCloud account so you can access them on your devices. Turning sync off leaves existing copies in iCloud. If you share a clip, it goes to the app or recipient you choose.

Deleting a ride

In Your rides, select the ride’s delete option. To remove a synced ride from iCloud and your synced devices, keep sync on until the deletion finishes. Delete any separate copies in Photos, Files or another app there as well.

TestFlight and support

During the beta, Apple automatically shares crash reports, device details and usage information with Knoet Studio. Apple also shares feedback and screenshots you submit. If you were invited by email, we can see your name and email address. We use this information to troubleshoot the beta. Apple explains how TestFlight handles and retains this data.

If you email us, we use your address and what you send to respond. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request. To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of information we hold about you, email knoetcontact@gmail.com.
